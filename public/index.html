How the score works

Each ETF is tested against its category benchmark and the risk-free rate (13-week T-bill yield). Every test scores +1, 0 or −1, is weighted, and the weighted mean is mapped onto 0–100. 65+ is Favorable, 35 or below is Unfavorable, anything between is Neutral.

    This is an analytical tool, not investment advice. Past performance does not predict future returns.
